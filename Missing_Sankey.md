# Missing Sankey Diagram

This Sankey diagram represents the synthesis stage of the project and was developed to explore the question:

**"Where is pressure applied?"**

The visual combines findings from the Economic, Education, Government & Policy, and Social Stratification analyses to illustrate the potential pathways through which pressure may be applied to mental health.

The diagram suggests that mental health pressure may emerge through multiple interacting mechanisms rather than a single factor. These include financial insecurity, educational transitions, waiting times, reduced access to support, deprivation, ethnic disparities, life-stage vulnerability, and social isolation.

As a synthesis visual, the diagram should be interpreted as a conceptual representation of patterns identified across the analyses rather than evidence of direct causal relationships.
